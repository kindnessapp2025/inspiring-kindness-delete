Account & Data Deletion

If you would like to delete your Inspiring Kindness account and associated data, please contact us using the details below.

Email: support@qgms.cloud

Please include the following information in your request:

  • Registered email address or username
  • Reason for deletion (optional)

What data will be deleted:

  • User account profile
  • Uploaded photos and videos
  • Associated user-generated content

Deletion timeline:
Requests are processed within 7–14 days.

If you have any questions, please contact us at the email above.
